THE PREMISE

One hut. One pond. Four forests that remember every tree you fell.

You wake at a small hut beside a still pond, the last quiet ground in a forest that has forgotten how to sleep. Around you, four woodlands press in, each carrying its own temper. Water holds the north. Fire burns to the south. Wind moves in the west. Earth stands patient and heavy to the east.

Every tree you chop feeds the hut and can drop saplings. Comfort has a cost. What you cut by daylight is what comes looking for you after dark. Some of those walking trees carry an element. Some do not. The only things standing between your hut and the treeline are an axe, a fire, and whatever luck the night decides to give you.

THE WORLD

Four zones, four tempers, one hub between them

NORTH

Water

Slow rivers and heavy roots. Trees grow tall and stubborn near the banks, and what grows back from their stumps moves in patient currents. Kill enough water trees at night and you carry that buff until dawn.

SOUTH

Fire

Dry timber and low embers. Trees here burn hot once they fall, and the saplings that follow do not wait patiently. Fire nights hit hard. Fire buffs hit harder if you can take the trees down first.

WEST

Wind

Open groves and leaning trunks. These trees give easily in the gusts, and what grows back from them moves fast. Wind enemies close the gap before you are ready unless you have already bound their element.

EAST

Earth

Dense groves and old growth. These trees take the most swings to bring down, and the ground itself seems to remember every one you take. Earth nights are slow, heavy, and stubborn.

CORE LOOP

Day builds it. Night tests it.

DAY

  • Pick a forest. Head north for water, south for fire, west for wind, or east for earth.
  • Chop by size. Small trees fall in one hit. Medium trees take two. Large trees take three.
  • Shake saplings loose. Each strike can drop a random number of saplings. Those saplings are what spawn the trees that walk at night.
  • Haul wood home. Fallen trees drop wood. Carry it back to patch and upgrade the hut.

NIGHT

  • Saplings wake up. When the sun sets, the day's saplings become tree enemies. Small, medium, and large, matching the hits it took to drop them. Some carry water, fire, wind, or earth. Some carry nothing.
  • They path to the hut. Hold the line. The hut is the only thing that matters after dark.
  • Steal a buff, or don't. Cut an elemental tree down and keep that element for the rest of the night. Two water trees dead means you carry water until dawn. Non-elemental trees still take hits. They just do not give you a buff.
  • Lucky night or not. Elemental saplings tend to sit opposite whatever you are most likely to have bound. Plain trees can fill the gaps. RNG can still tilt a night easy, or make it a grind.

TREES AND ENEMIES

One chop. Two chop. Three chop.

Logging should feel good. The same three sizes that make chopping satisfying also make the night readable. You always know how many hits something will take, whether it carries an element or not.

1

SMALL

One swing to fell. One swing to kill when it comes back at night.

2

MEDIUM

Two swings by day. Two hits to put it down after dark.

3

LARGE

Three swings, and a night enemy that takes three hits to drop.

Elemental or not, the hit count stays the same. Water, fire, wind, and earth trees can grant a night buff. Plain trees cannot.

THE BOSS TREE

Once in a while a boss sapling falls off a trunk. It takes root. It grows over a stretch of days. Cutting it down is a job of its own. Fighting it that night is the real fight, a boss battle built from the same loop you have been living in.

KEY FEATURES

What you are actually doing, hour to hour

TIERED CHOPPING

Small, medium, and large trees each demand a different number of hits, so every grove rewards reading the treeline before you swing.

SAPLING CONSEQUENCE

Strikes can drop a random handful of saplings. Those saplings become the night's enemies, so your own chopping writes the fight you have to survive.

LUCKY AND UNLUCKY NIGHTS

Elemental saplings tend to sit opposite whatever you are most likely to have enchanted. Plain trees can fill a night without tilting the matchup. Chance can still swing a night easy, or load it until it hurts.

NIGHT BUFFS

Kill the walking trees of one element and carry that buff until dawn. Stack a couple of the same kind and the rest of the night changes shape. Plain trees do not grant a buff.

HUT REPAIR

Wood collected during the day patches damage from the night before and funds upgrades that keep the hub standing longer.

FOUR DISTINCT ZONES

Water to the north, fire to the south, wind to the west, and earth to the east. Each one has its own pacing and night behavior. Non-elemental trees can show up in any of them.

BOSS SAPLINGS

A rare sapling takes root and grows over days. Chopping it down is an event. Fighting it that night is a boss battle.

A HUB WORTH DEFENDING

The pond and hut at the center of the map are small, personal, and yours. Every night has something concrete to protect.

PLAIN TREES

Not every sapling carries a temper. Non-elemental enemies still come in small, medium, and large. They take the same hits. They just do not give you water, fire, wind, or earth when they fall.

CRAFTING, LATER

A crafting expansion is on the table if players ask for it enough. For now the loop is chop, survive, and make logging feel as good as it should.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Built in Godot 4, in active development

Chopper is in early development. The hub, the four zones, and the day and night cycle are being built and tested. Sapling drops, night buffs, and boss growth are in progress. No release date has been set.
