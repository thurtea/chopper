Chopper

Chop trees during the day. At night the saplings you knocked loose become enemies and walk toward your hut.

The game

You live at a hut next to a pond. Four forests sit around it. North is water, south is fire, west is wind, east is earth.

Wood from the trees goes back to the hut for repairs and upgrades. Hitting a tree can drop saplings. Those saplings are what show up as enemies after dark.

Some of the walking trees have an element. Kill enough of one kind and you keep that buff until morning. Some have no element. They still take hits. They just don't give a buff.

Once in a while a boss sapling drops. It takes root and grows over a few days. Cutting it down is a job. Fighting it that night is the boss fight.

Crafting might show up later if people ask for it. Not part of the first build.

Day and night

Day

  • Pick a forest and chop.
  • Small trees take one hit. Medium take two. Large take three.
  • Each swing can drop a random number of saplings.
  • Carry wood home to patch the hut.

Night

  • The day's saplings become tree enemies and path to the hut.
  • Hit counts match the tree size: one, two, or three.
  • Kill elemental trees to hold that element until dawn.
  • Nights can come out easy or ugly depending on what spawned.

Status

The 3D Unity version is the one being built. First pass is the chopping loop: walk up to a tree, click, the tree takes damage and goes away when health hits zero. Greybox only, primitives, no art yet.

A 2D version is planned later. That will likely be Unity as well.
